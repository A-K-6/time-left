Time Left is developed and maintained by Aeen Karimian.

Calendar access
If you allow calendar access, Time Left reads calendar and account labels, event titles, descriptions, times, and status from Android’s calendar provider. It uses them to show your calendar, suggest countdowns, and match quiet rules. When quiet rules are enabled, the app checks matching events in the background. Time Left does not edit calendar events or sign in to your calendar account. Your calendar provider manages its own sync.

Notification and app access
If you enable notification snoozing, Android can deliver other apps’ notifications to Time Left, including when Time Left is closed. Time Left uses the sending app, notification identifier, and type to snooze eligible notifications from the apps you select during active quiet rules. Time Left does not read or save notification message text. When you open the app picker, Time Left reads names of apps with launch icons so you can choose which apps to snooze. Already snoozed notifications keep their scheduled return time if you pause or change a rule.

Widgets and alerts
If you add a widget, Time Left sends the widget host generic timer labels, countdown timing, and timer controls. Widget labels omit calendar titles and custom focus labels. Android notifications can show detailed timer labels and calendar reminder titles. Android’s notification and lock screen settings control when that information is visible.

Local storage and sharing
Time Left stores your rules, calendar selections, timer history and labels, focus progress, settings, and the state needed for automation in private app storage. Recent completion records are periodically pruned. Focus totals, preferences, and rules remain until you remove them or clear the app’s storage. Android backups and device transfer are disabled for this app. Time Left has no account, analytics, ads, or server and does not send this data off your device.

Your controls
You can turn off quiet rules in the app and revoke calendar or notification access in Android Settings. Revoking calendar access stops new snoozes. Already snoozed notifications keep their scheduled return time. Clearing Time Left’s storage in Android Settings or uninstalling the app removes its locally saved data.

Privacy questions
Use the support contact on the store listing where you obtained Time Left to reach Aeen Karimian with privacy questions. Please leave calendar and notification details out of public reports.
